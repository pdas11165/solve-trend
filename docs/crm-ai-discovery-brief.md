# Custom CRM + AI Automation — Discovery Call Brief

_A working sheet for scoping a custom CRM / AI automation engagement. Bring it to the call, take notes inline, and by the end you should be able to size the project into a phase 1._

---

## 0. The one decision that sets everything else: build vs. configure

Before anything, you're trying to figure out which of these the client actually needs:

| Path | What it means | Rough scope | Best when |
|------|---------------|-------------|-----------|
| **Configure a platform** | Set up + customize HubSpot, Pipedrive, Attio, Airtable, etc., and automate on top | Days → 2–3 weeks | They have a fairly standard process, small team, want speed and low maintenance |
| **Hybrid (platform + custom layer)** | Keep a platform for what it's good at; build a custom dashboard / integration / AI layer on top | 2–6 weeks | They mostly fit a platform but have 1–2 things it can't do |
| **Fully custom CRM** | Own database + UI built around their exact pipeline | 6+ weeks, ongoing | Their process doesn't fit any platform, or they've outgrown one |

**Your default recommendation for a "convert now" client:** steer to **configure or hybrid for v1.** It's faster, cheaper to prove value, and turns into a recurring retainer. Reserve full-custom for when a platform is demonstrably in the way. You can always graduate them to custom later.

> Your job on the call isn't to sell the biggest build — it's to find the smallest v1 that solves a real problem and sets up recurring work.

## 1. Business & process (understand before you tool)

- What does your business do, and who's on the team that would use this?
- Walk me through your sales process today, stage by stage. What happens from "new lead" to "closed"?
- What tool(s) are you using right now — a CRM, a spreadsheet, your inbox, nothing?
- Where does time leak today? What's the most annoying, repetitive part?
- If this project were a total success 6 months from now, what's different?

## 2. Data & pipeline (what it tracks)

- What are you tracking — contacts, companies, deals, projects, something else?
- What are your pipeline stages, in your words?
- What info do you need on a contact/deal that your current setup can't hold?
- Roughly how many records? (Hundreds? Thousands? Tens of thousands?)

## 3. Integrations (where scope creep hides)

- What tools do you live in day to day? (Email, calendar, phone/SMS, invoicing, payments, docs, Slack…)
- Which of those need to talk to the CRM?
- Where does data get entered twice today?
- Any tool that's non-negotiable and must stay?

> ⚠️ Every integration is scope. Nail down which are **v1 must-haves** vs. "nice later."

## 4. The AI automation layer (pick 1–2 for v1)

Candidates — ask which would save the most time:

- **Lead enrichment** — auto-pull context on a new contact
- **Call / email summaries** — turn conversations into a few action lines
- **Follow-up drafting** — AI drafts, human edits and sends
- **Lead scoring & routing** — surface the deals worth attention
- **AI assistant / chatbot** — answer FAQs or query their own data
- **Data entry / cleanup** — parse messy input into structured records

Questions:
- Of these, which one would you notice if it disappeared tomorrow?
- How is that task done today, and how long does it take?
- What has to stay a human decision? (Keep AI on prep, not the final call.)

## 5. Users, roles & access

- Who uses it — just you, or a team?
- Does everyone see everything, or do roles need different access?
- Any approvals or handoffs between people?

## 6. Data migration

- Is there existing data to bring in? From where, and how clean is it?
- Does history matter, or is a fresh start fine?

## 7. Hosting, support & the recurring relationship

- After launch, who maintains it? (This is your retainer.)
- Expectations on uptime, changes, new features over time?
- Comfortable with a monthly support/iteration arrangement?

## 8. Timeline & budget (ask, don't assume)

- Is there a date driving this? What happens if it slips?
- Do you have a budget range in mind, or should I propose options?
- Would you rather a bigger build up front, or a lean v1 we grow?

## Scoping framework — turn answers into a phase 1

After the call, sort everything into three buckets:

- **v1 (now)** — the core objects + pipeline, the one integration that removes double-entry, and the single highest-value AI automation. This is what you quote and start.
- **v2 (next)** — the second automation, more integrations, roles/permissions, reporting.
- **Later / maybe** — everything that sounded cool but nobody would miss.

**Rough effort tiers** (calibrate to your own rates):

| Tier | Looks like | Ballpark |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Small | Configure a platform + 1 automation + light branding | ~1–2 weeks |
| Medium | Hybrid: platform + custom dashboard/integration + 1–2 AI automations | ~3–6 weeks |
| Large | Custom-built CRM, multiple integrations, AI layer, migration | 6+ weeks + retainer |

## Scope-creep watch-outs

- "Can it also just…" → write it down as v2, don't fold it into v1.
- Vague integrations ("connect everything") → force a specific list.
- Automating a process nobody has actually defined → define the manual version first.
- Custom-building something a platform already does well → question it.

## After the call — your next move

1. Send a short recap: what you heard, the proposed v1, and what's parked for later.
2. Put a number on v1 (fixed scope) + a monthly support option.
3. Offer a paid discovery/blueprint step if the scope is still fuzzy — it de-risks both sides and is itself billable.
