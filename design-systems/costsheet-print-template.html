Running costs

Three accounts keep the system running. All three open in your name, on your card, at cost. I take no margin on any of it and none of it appears on my invoices. Prices are in United States dollars.

Supabase

Database and sign in

Free Start here

$0

Fine for what you need. 500 MB of storage, which is far more than 137 farms will fill.

Pro

$25

A backup taken every day and kept for a week, it never sleeps, and support answers by email.

Start on Free. Two things worth knowing for later: it sleeps after seven days with nobody signed in, and it keeps no backups. Neither matters while you are getting used to the system. Once it is holding work you would not want to lose, Pro is the sensible move, and it is a single click.

Where it runs

Runs like an ordinary server. Never sleeps, no monthly allowance to run out of. You pay for what it uses.

Where the preview runs today, so nothing needs moving. Free covers roughly fifteen gigabytes a month.

Either works. Railway is a few dollars and behaves predictably. Netlify is free to begin with, though the free plan stops serving once the monthly allowance is used, and the paid plan at nine dollars removes that. Moving between them is included in the fee.
